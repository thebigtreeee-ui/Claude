# Fotorealistische Bild-Prompts – Palast an der Elbe

Für Bildgeneratoren wie Midjourney, ChatGPT/DALL·E, Higgsfield oder Flux.
Tipp: Lade zusätzlich das passende Render aus `renders/` als Referenzbild hoch, damit Form und Lage stimmen.

**Grundbeschreibung (bei jedem Prompt voranstellen):**
Photorealistic architectural photograph of a monumental oval ring palace, 600 x 700 m, built on a green meander peninsula of the river Elbe near Dessau-Rosslau, Germany. The ring is a continuous two-storey building in warm cream sandstone with arched shop windows on the ground floor, a balustraded rooftop terrace with white parasols, potted palms and restaurants, and 20 slender round towers with cream domes and gold finials. Main gate in the south with two tall towers and gilded domes, a stone bridge crossing the Elbe. Inside: a formal park with radial gravel avenues, a large baroque palace with a slate dome in the north, a central fountain plaza. Surroundings: German floodplain meadows, riverside forests, the industrial harbour of Rosslau in the north.

1. **Luftbild Abend (Drohne von Südost):** Drone shot at golden hour from 350 m height, south-east, the whole oval visible, warm lights switched on along the terrace, river reflecting the orange sky, realistic haze, shot on DJI Mavic 3, 24 mm, ultra detailed.
2. **Blick vom Kornhaus (Tag):** Eye-level view from the southern Elbe bank at the Bauhaus Kornhaus Dessau, summer midday, the ring palace across the water, people on the riverside path, natural colours, 35 mm lens.
3. **Haupttor bei Nacht:** Night photograph from the bridge looking at the illuminated main gate, gilded domes lit from below, warm glowing shop arches, lanterns along the bridge, pedestrians, long exposure, wet stone reflections, 24 mm.
4. **Dachterrasse Sonnenuntergang:** View along the curving rooftop terrace at sunset, guests at restaurant tables under white parasols, palms, string lights, the Elbe and the floodplain forest beyond the balustrade, lifestyle photography, 35 mm, shallow depth of field.
5. **Palast & Brunnen (Tag):** Wide shot across the central fountain plaza towards the baroque palace, flower beds, families walking, summer afternoon, crisp detail, 24 mm.
6. **Fassade vom Fluss (Dämmerung):** Low drone shot above the Elbe at blue hour, the outer facade with lit arched windows mirrored in calm water, riverside trees, a small yacht.
7. **Luftbild Nord:** High drone view from the north over the Rosslau harbour towards the ring palace, afternoon light, the river bend wrapping around the site.
8. **Draufsicht:** Straight top-down satellite-style photo of the oval palace and park inside the Elbe meander, realistic textures, early evening.

**Negativ-Prompt (falls unterstützt):** cartoon, 3d render, low poly, plastic, distorted architecture, extra towers, text, watermark
